12-calendar-month retention rate
The percentage of placed candidates who remain continuously employed by the hiring company through their 12-calendar-month start-date anniversary. Only hires whose anniversary has occurred as of the reporting end date are eligible. Employees leaving before their anniversary are not retained; newer hires are pending and excluded from the denominator.
