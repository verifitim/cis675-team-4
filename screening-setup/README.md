# Screening Setup — US1

Hiring managers select or write interview questions, edit equal-weight 0–2 scoring rubrics, save drafts, and approve a job-specific setup. Supports the saved Job Posting and the sample Training Coordinator job.

Approval requires at least one question, nonblank question text and all three rubric descriptions, plus explicit confirmation. Editing or removing a question, adding a question, or changing a rubric invalidates approval immediately. Drafts may be incomplete. Switching jobs saves the current state. Data and approval are browser-local, not shared across users or devices. Clearing browser storage removes them.

This is a functioning configuration-and-approval prototype using fictional data. It does not conduct interviews, automatically score answers, verify hiring suitability, or provide a server-side screening gate. Approval represents the intended prerequisite for future screening. Questions and evidence may draw on work, projects, or volunteering to support transferable abilities as well as conventional qualifications.

## Acceptance checks

1. Add a sample question; edit its wording and rubric. Remove it, then add it again.
2. Add a custom question. Approval must fail until all three rubric descriptions are completed.
3. Approval must fail with no questions or without the confirmation checkbox.
4. Save an incomplete draft, refresh, and verify it remains.
5. Complete the setup, check confirmation, approve, refresh, and verify Approved remains.
6. Edit an approved question or rubric, add or remove a question: status must become Draft and require reapproval, including after refresh.
7. Switch jobs and verify independent questions and approvals.
8. Verify Screening Setup appears in the hiring manager page selector on all five pages, and all navigation destinations exist.
